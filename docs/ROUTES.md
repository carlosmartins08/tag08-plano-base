# Routes and anchors

- / redirects to /pt
- /pt, /en, /es, /fr are the published localized landing pages
- /llms.txt, /robots.txt, /sitemap.xml, /api/geo

Anchors: #hero, #problema, #solucao, #pilares, #ciclo, #fit, #criterios, #investimento, #faq, #contato.

Each locale has its own canonical URL and the common hreflang set, including x-default to /pt.
